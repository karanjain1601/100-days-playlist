The seven phases

Phase 1 · Days 1–16 · 5h 19m

Foundations & Text Processing

Take any raw messy corpus and turn it into clean, tokenized, normalized data — without touching machine learning yet.
Phase 2 · Days 17–36 · 7h 24m

Classical & Statistical NLP

Build, tune and honestly evaluate a text classifier and a sequence tagger with pre-neural methods.
Phase 3 · Days 37–52 · 6h 00m

Semantics, Topics & Embeddings

How meaning gets encoded as vectors — and a working semantic search engine.
Phase 4 · Days 53–70 · 7h 14m

Neural Networks for NLP

PyTorch fluency, recurrence and attention from the ground up, and a trained seq2seq model.
Phase 5 · Days 71–88 · 7h 32m

Transformers

Write a transformer from scratch, train your own tokenizer, fine-tune BERT-family models.
Phase 6 · Days 89–108 · 8h 20m

Large Language Models

How modern LLMs are built, adapted, prompted, grounded and evaluated — plus a production RAG system.
Phase 7 · Days 109–120 · 4h 50m

Applied, Production & Career

Notebook to production, the specialized subfields, and a portfolio plus a plan.

How to read a lesson

Each day carries a runtime target, the sub-topics that must appear, and the artifact the viewer builds. The notes here expand each of those into something you can study from directly — or produce a video from.

The standard video timeline

Use this internal structure for every lesson so the playlist feels consistent. Percentages scale to whatever the target runtime is.

SegmentSharePurpose
Cold open / problem hook5%Show the broken thing before the fix
Recap of yesterday5%One slide; keeps the series stitched together
Concept + intuition30%Whiteboard or animation, no code yet
Math or algorithm walkthrough20%Only where the lesson has one
Live code30%Screen recording, typed not pasted
Recap + tomorrow's teaser10%Plus the day's exercise on screen
Post these as chapters Retention on long-form tutorials depends heavily on chapter navigation. Chapters are mandatory on every upload.

Appendix A · Recurring formats

Slot these between phases as bonus uploads for extra volume without breaking the numbering.

Paper in 10
One seminal paper per week, ten minutes each. The Day 118 reading list is the queue.
Debug This
A broken NLP notebook; viewers find the bug before the reveal. The pitfall boxes in these notes are the source material.
Model Card Reviews
Read a real model card and critique it against the Day 36 checklist.
Ask Me Anything
Monthly Q&A pulled from comments on the phase just finished.
