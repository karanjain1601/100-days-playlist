How every lesson is built

  • Cold open the result or failure that makes the paper matter
  • The problem what the field could not do the day before
  • The method the equations, derived step by step
  • Evidence the key experiments and what they do and don't show
  • Limits & what broke failed replications, buried ablations, successors
  • Bridge how it connects to the next paper, plus reading questions
