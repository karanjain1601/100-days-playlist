100 Days of AI Engineering

Building products on foundation models, in 133 lessons: prompts and context, embeddings and search, retrieval-augmented generation, tools and MCP, agents, evals, security, production operations and fine-tuning open models. Every lesson explains the mechanism, shows Python you can run, says what breaks in production, and adds a piece to one running project.

Phases

13 phases, in order. Each page holds every lesson of its phase.

PHASE 0 · DAYS 1–4

Orientation and the LLM API

Phase 0 builds the workbench the rest of the course stands on: a reproducible Python project, one logged door for every model call, and the habit of measuring before changing anything.

6 lessons · 4h 00m video PHASE 1 · DAYS 5–11

How LLMs Work, for Engineers

This phase teaches just enough of how language models work inside to make sound engineering decisions: why long prompts are slow, why output tokens cost more, why temperature 0 still varies, what reasoning models spend their tokens on, and where hallucinations

10 lessons · 7h 25m video PHASE 2 · DAYS 12–19

Prompt and Context Engineering

Phase 2 turns prompts into engineered artefacts: structured, schema-checked, versioned, tested, cached and composed into fixed workflows.

11 lessons · 8h 10m video PHASE 3 · DAYS 20–26

Embeddings and Search

Phase 3 builds DocPilot's retrieval layer from first principles and measures every choice: embeddings, BM25, approximate nearest-neighbour indexes, vector databases, metadata filters, hybrid fusion, reranking and embedding fine-tuning.

10 lessons · 7h 30m video PHASE 4 · DAYS 27–35

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Phase 4 turns the Phase 3 search service into an assistant that answers questions from the Kubernetes docs with sources a reader can check.

12 lessons · 9h 00m video PHASE 5 · DAYS 36–43

Advanced RAG and RAG Evaluation

Phase 5 turns DocPilot's working RAG pipeline into one you can measure, debug and extend: retrieval and generation are scored separately, failures are traced to the stage that caused them, and advanced techniques are adopted only when the numbers say they help

11 lessons · 8h 25m video PHASE 6 · DAYS 44–49

Tool Use and the Model Context Protocol

Phase 6 gives DocPilot hands: function calling from first principles, tools designed for a model to use, sandboxed code execution, web access, MCP servers and computer use.

9 lessons · 6h 40m video PHASE 7 · DAYS 50–61

Agents

Phase 7 turns DocPilot's tool loop into agents that plan, remember, survive crashes and ask before they act.

16 lessons · 12h 15m video PHASE 8 · DAYS 62–69

Evaluation and Testing

Phase 8 turns DocPilot's quality from an impression into a measurement: versioned eval datasets, deterministic checks, a model judge calibrated against human labels, agent evaluations with repeated runs, and CI gates fed by production feedback.

10 lessons · 7h 25m video PHASE 9 · DAYS 70–75

Security, Safety and Guardrails

Phase 9 threat-models DocPilot, attacks it with prompt-injection and red-team suites, and defends it by design rather than by prompt.

8 lessons · 6h 05m video PHASE 10 · DAYS 76–84

Production LLMOps

Starts with Architecture of an LLM Application in Production; Streaming to Users: SSE, WebSockets and Partial Results; Observability: Traces, Spans and GenAI Semantic Conventions.

11 lessons · 8h 10m video PHASE 11 · DAYS 85–92

Open Models, Fine-Tuning and Serving

This phase is about owning the model: choosing open weights you are allowed to use, running them locally and at scale, and fine-tuning a small model until it can take over a job from the API.

10 lessons · 7h 45m video PHASE 12 · DAYS 93–100

Frontier Patterns and Capstone

Phase 12 covers the newest patterns in AI engineering (voice agents, long-running agents, skills and generative interfaces) and then a four-day capstone that combines the whole course into one deployed, evaluated system.

9 lessons · 5h 40m video

How every lesson is built

  • Core idea the one sentence the lesson exists to teach
  • How it works the mechanism underneath the API or the pattern
  • Code runnable Python for every technique in the plan
  • Diagrams pipelines, agent loops, request flows and trade-off curves
  • What breaks the failure modes you will meet in production
  • Build the practice task that adds to the DocPilot project
