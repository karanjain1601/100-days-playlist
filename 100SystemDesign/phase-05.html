D067

Why Distributed Systems Are Hard

26 min · Phase 05 · Prereq: Day 66

What the lesson covers

  • The 8 fallacies of distributed computing, each with a real outage
  • Partial failure vs total failure
  • The impossibility of distinguishing a slow node from a dead one
  • Unbounded message delays
  • Independent vs correlated failures
  • The "it worked on one machine" trap
  • How failure probability compounds with node count
Whiteboard. 1 node at 99.9% → 100 nodes: what's the probability that something is broken right now?
HomeworkList every failure mode in a 3-service call chain (there are more than 15).
