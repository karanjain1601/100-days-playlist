D057

Load Balancing Fundamentals

30 min · Phase 04 · Prereq: Day 56

What the lesson covers

  • L4 vs L7 balancing (what each can and can't see)
  • DSR and connection handling
  • Hardware vs software vs cloud LBs
  • ALB/NLB/GLB comparison
  • TLS termination placement
  • Connection multiplexing to backends
  • The LB as a bottleneck and a SPOF
  • DNS LB vs anycast vs hardware LB tiers in a real edge stack
Whiteboard. a full edge architecture — anycast → L4 → L7 → services.
HomeworkPut an L7 proxy in front of 3 backends with path- and header-based routing.
D058

Load Balancing Algorithms

28 min · Phase 04 · Prereq: Day 57

What the lesson covers

  • Round robin, weighted RR, least connections, least response time, random, power-of-two-choices (and why it's near-optimal), source IP hash, consistent hashing, maglev hashing
  • Heterogeneous backends
  • Slow-start for newly added instances
  • Algorithm impact on tail latency
  • How "least connections" fails with long-lived connections
Whiteboard. simulated request distribution — RR vs least-conn vs P2C under uneven load.
HomeworkSimulate 10K requests across all algorithms and chart p99 for each.
D059

Health Checks, Failover & LB High Availability

28 min · Phase 04 · Prereq: Day 58

What the lesson covers

  • Active vs passive health checks
  • Shallow vs deep checks and the cascading-failure risk of deep checks
  • Check intervals, thresholds, and flapping
  • Outlier detection and ejection
  • Connection draining and graceful shutdown (SIGTERM handling, preStop)
  • Sticky sessions and how to avoid needing them
  • LB redundancy with VRRP/keepalived, floating IPs, and anycast
  • The "health check took down the fleet" incident pattern
Whiteboard. a deep health check that fails every instance simultaneously — and the safe redesign.
HomeworkImplement readiness vs liveness correctly and test rolling restarts with zero errors.
D060

Nginx, HAProxy & Envoy in Practice

30 min · Phase 04 · Prereq: Day 59

What the lesson covers

  • Nginx worker/event architecture, upstreams, buffering, timeouts that actually matter
  • HAProxy modes, stick tables, observability
  • Envoy's model — listeners, filter chains, clusters, endpoints, xDS dynamic config
  • Retries, timeouts, outlier detection, circuit breaking at the proxy layer
  • Picking one for a given job
  • Config anti-patterns that cause outages
Whiteboard. annotated production config with every timeout labeled and justified.
HomeworkConfigure retries + circuit breaking in Envoy and prove it with fault injection.

Service Discovery

  • Client-side vs server-side discovery
  • Registry patterns — self-registration vs third-party registration
  • Consul, etcd, Eureka, ZooKeeper compared
  • Kubernetes DNS, Services, Endpoints/EndpointSlices

D062

Autoscaling

30 min · Phase 04 · Prereq: Day 61

What the lesson covers

  • Horizontal vs vertical vs cluster autoscaling
  • Metric selection (CPU is usually wrong — use QPS, queue depth, concurrency, or latency)
  • Target tracking vs step vs scheduled vs predictive scaling
  • Cooldowns, thrashing, and hysteresis
  • Scale-up speed vs instance boot time
  • Warm pools and pre-provisioning
  • Scale-to-zero and cold starts
  • Autoscaling stateful systems
  • The cost/latency tradeoff curve
Whiteboard. a traffic spike vs an autoscaler's response curve — showing the gap where users suffer.
HomeworkConfigure queue-depth-based autoscaling and test with a spike.
D063

Backpressure, Load Shedding & Queue Theory

32 min · Phase 04 · Prereq: Day 62

What the lesson covers

  • Little's Law (L = λW) applied to thread pools and queues
  • Why unbounded queues are a bug
  • Bounded queues and rejection
  • Backpressure propagation across services and in streaming
  • Admission control and priority-based shedding (drop cheap traffic first)
  • Concurrency limiting (adaptive, TCP-Vegas-style)
  • The difference between failing fast and failing slow
  • Queueing delay as the hidden cause of p99 blowups
Whiteboard. utilization vs latency curve — the hockey stick past 70–80%.
HomeworkAdd a bounded queue plus shedding to a service and prove p99 stability under overload.

D066

Multi-Region & Multi-AZ Architecture

34 min · Phase 04 · Prereq: Day 65

What the lesson covers

  • AZ vs region failure domains
  • Active-passive, active-active, and pilot-light patterns with cost/RTO comparison
  • Data replication across regions and the consistency ceiling set by physics
  • Write routing (home region, global writes, conflict resolution)
