D141

The Interview Framework

34 min · Phase 10 · Prereq: Day 140

What the lesson covers

  • The 45-minute time budget by phase
  • Asking scoping questions that reveal seniority
  • Stating assumptions out loud
  • Driving the whiteboard instead of waiting
  • When to go deep vs stay broad
  • Signaling tradeoff thinking
  • Recovering from a wrong turn
  • What interviewers actually score (structure, tradeoffs, depth, communication, correctness)
  • Differences by level (mid vs senior vs staff)
HomeworkRun a full design in 45 minutes with a timer, out loud, recorded.
D142

Low-Level Design & Object Modeling

36 min · Phase 10 · Prereq: Day 141

What the lesson covers

  • LLD vs HLD in interviews
  • Class design, SOLID applied without dogma
  • The design patterns that actually appear (strategy, factory, observer, state, decorator, adapter)
  • Concurrency in LLD (thread safety, immutability)
  • Designing a parking lot, an elevator, a rate limiter class, and a card game — with the modeling reasoning shown
  • Writing clean interfaces and testable seams
HomeworkModel two classic LLD problems and defend every class boundary.
D143

Tradeoff Drills & Follow-Up Questions

32 min · Phase 10 · Prereq: Day 142

What the lesson covers

  • 40 rapid-fire tradeoff prompts with model answers (SQL vs NoSQL here, cache vs no cache, sync vs async, strong vs eventual, monolith vs services, push vs pull, normalize vs denormalize)
  • The follow-up patterns interviewers reuse ("10× traffic", "one region dies", "make it cheaper", "make it real-time")
  • How to answer "it depends" without saying "it depends"
  • Handling questions you don't know
HomeworkAnswer 20 drills in 60 seconds each, out loud.
D144

Full Mock Interview, Unedited

40 min · Phase 10 · Prereq: Day 143

What the lesson covers

  • Live commentary track on what scored well and what didn't
  • The interviewer's scoring rubric filled in at the end
  • The three most common failure patterns (jumping to a solution, ignoring requirements, going too deep too early)
HomeworkSelf-score your Day 141 recording against the same rubric.

Capstone & How to Keep Growing

  • How to read papers and engineering blogs productively
  • A curated reading list (Dynamo, Bigtable, Spanner, Kafka, Raft, MapReduce, Chubby, Borg, Zanzibar, Monarch)
  • Building system-design skills at work through design reviews
  • The 12-month path after this playlist
