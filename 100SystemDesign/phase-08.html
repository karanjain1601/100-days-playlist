Reliability, Observability, Security & Cost

D109

Observability I: Metrics

32 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 108

What the lesson covers

  • The three pillars and why metrics come first
  • RED (rate, errors, duration) for services and USE (utilization, saturation, errors) for resources
  • Counters, gauges, histograms, summaries
  • Percentile calculation from histograms and why you can't average percentiles
  • Cardinality explosions and label discipline
  • Prometheus architecture — scraping, PromQL, recording rules, remote write, federation
  • Push vs pull
  • Dashboards that answer "is it broken and where"
Whiteboard. a service dashboard built from scratch with exactly the panels that matter.
HomeworkInstrument an endpoint with RED metrics and build a 4-panel dashboard.
D110

Observability II: Logging at Scale

28 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 109

What the lesson covers

  • Structured logging and consistent field schemas
  • Log levels used correctly
  • Correlation/request IDs threaded through every hop
  • Sampling strategies for high-volume paths
  • PII scrubbing and compliance
  • The ELK/OpenSearch and Loki pipelines — ingestion, indexing, retention tiers, cost
  • Logs vs events vs metrics (when a log should have been a metric)
  • Log-based alerting pitfalls
  • The cost of logging everything at 1M QPS
Whiteboard. one request's logs stitched across 6 services by trace ID.
HomeworkAdd structured logging with correlation IDs and trace one request end to end.
D111

Observability III: Distributed Tracing

30 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 110

What the lesson covers

  • Traces, spans, parent-child relationships, and span attributes
  • Context propagation (W3C traceparent) across HTTP, gRPC, and message queues
  • OpenTelemetry — SDK, collector, exporters, and vendor neutrality
  • Head vs tail sampling and how to keep the interesting traces
  • Service dependency maps generated from traces
  • Critical path analysis and finding the real bottleneck in a fan-out
  • Tracing async and batched work
  • Trace-driven debugging of p99s
Whiteboard. a 12-span trace where the slow span isn't the one you'd guess.
HomeworkInstrument two services with OTel and find the latency contributor from a trace.
D112

Alerting, On-Call & Incident Response

30 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 111

What the lesson covers

  • Symptom-based vs cause-based alerts
  • Alerting on SLO burn rate (multi-window, multi-burn-rate) instead of thresholds
  • Alert fatigue, noise budgets, and deleting alerts
  • Severity levels and paging policy
  • Runbooks that are actually usable at 3 a.m
  • The incident command model — roles, comms, status updates
  • Mitigate first, diagnose later
  • Blameless postmortems, contributing factors, and action-item follow-through
  • MTTD/MTTA/MTTR as improvement targets
Whiteboard. an incident timeline from first alert to postmortem action items.
HomeworkConvert 5 threshold alerts into SLO burn-rate alerts and delete the noisy ones.
D113

Capacity Planning & Performance Testing

32 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 112

What the lesson covers

  • Modeling demand (organic growth, seasonality, launches, marketing spikes)
  • Headroom targets and why 100% utilization is a myth
  • Single-instance benchmarking to find the per-node ceiling
  • Load vs stress vs soak vs spike testing
  • Test design (realistic mixes, think time, warm caches vs cold)
  • K6/Gatling/Locust mechanics
  • Finding the knee in the latency curve
  • Profiling — CPU flame graphs, allocation, lock contention
  • Performance regression gates in CI
Whiteboard. a load-test curve identifying the exact saturation point and its bottleneck resource.
HomeworkLoad-test a service to failure and name the first resource that saturated.
D114

Chaos Engineering

28 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 113

What the lesson covers

  • The hypothesis-driven method (steady state → hypothesis → experiment → blast radius → learn)
  • Failure injection types — instance kill, latency, packet loss, dependency failure, resource exhaustion, clock skew, zone outage
  • Blast radius control and abort conditions
  • Running experiments in production safely
  • Game days and their facilitation
  • Chaos as a way to validate runbooks, not just code
  • Tooling (Chaos Monkey, Litmus, Gremlin, fault injection in meshes)
Whiteboard. a game-day plan for a regional AZ outage with abort criteria.
HomeworkKill a dependency in staging and document every user-visible symptom.
D115

High Availability Design

30 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 114

What the lesson covers

  • Identifying single points of failure systematically
  • Redundancy models — N+1, N+2, 2N, and their cost
  • Active-active vs active-passive per component
  • Failure domains and blast-radius reduction (cells, shuffle sharding)
  • Graceful degradation tiers and static fallbacks
  • Dependency criticality classification (hard vs soft dependencies) and removing hard ones
  • Availability math for series and parallel components
  • The availability ceiling set by your worst dependency
Whiteboard. an architecture reviewed component by component for SPOFs, with fixes ranked by cost.
HomeworkDraw your system and circle every SPOF; propose the cheapest fix for each.
D116

Backup & Disaster Recovery

30 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 115

What the lesson covers

  • RTO and RPO defined and negotiated with the business
  • Backup types — full, incremental, differential, snapshot, continuous/PITR
  • Application-consistent vs crash-consistent backups
  • The 3-2-1 rule, offsite, and immutable/air-gapped backups against ransomware
  • Retention policy and cost tiering
  • Restore testing (a backup you haven't restored isn't a backup)
  • DR strategies — backup/restore, pilot light, warm standby, hot multi-site — with cost/RTO comparison
  • Runbook rehearsal and failback
Whiteboard. RTO/RPO plotted against cost for four DR strategies.
HomeworkPerform a full restore drill and record the actual RTO you achieved.
D117

Security I: Authentication & Authorization

36 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 116

What the lesson covers

  • Authentication vs authorization vs identity
  • Password storage (bcrypt/scrypt/Argon2, salting, peppering), MFA, WebAuthn/passkeys
  • Sessions vs tokens
  • JWT structure, signing algorithms, validation mistakes (alg: none, unverified claims), expiry and the revocation problem, refresh token rotation
  • OAuth 2.0/2.1 grant flows with PKCE and what each is for
  • OpenID Connect vs SAML
  • SSO and federation
  • Authorization models — RBAC, ABAC, ReBAC (Zanzibar-style) — and centralized policy engines
  • Service-to-service auth with mTLS and workload identity
Whiteboard. a full OAuth authorization-code + PKCE flow, then the same flow with 3 planted vulnerabilities.
HomeworkImplement token refresh with rotation and reuse detection.
D118

Security II: Attack Surface & OWASP for Architects

32 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 117

What the lesson covers

  • Threat modeling with STRIDE and data-flow diagrams
  • Injection (SQL, NoSQL, command, template) and parameterization
  • Broken access control and IDOR (the most common real breach)
  • SSRF and its cloud-metadata escalation
  • XSS/CSRF and modern defenses (CSP, SameSite)
  • Insecure deserialization
  • Dependency and supply-chain risk (SBOM, pinning, provenance)
  • Secrets in code and history
  • Security headers
  • Defense in depth and the principle of least privilege
  • DDoS mitigation layers
  • WAF and bot management
  • Abuse and fraud as an architectural concern
Whiteboard. a threat model for an upload endpoint with 8 concrete abuse paths.
HomeworkThreat-model one of your endpoints and write mitigations for the top 3 risks.
D119

Security III: Data Protection & Compliance

32 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 118

What the lesson covers

  • Encryption in transit vs at rest vs in use
  • Envelope encryption and key hierarchies
  • KMS/HSM, key rotation, and crypto-shredding for deletion
  • Field-level encryption and searchable encryption tradeoffs
  • Tokenization and PCI scope reduction
  • PII classification, data mapping, and minimization
  • GDPR/CCPA obligations that change architecture (right to erasure, portability, residency)
  • Audit logging and tamper evidence
  • Secure multi-tenancy and data isolation proofs
  • Retention and deletion pipelines
Whiteboard. a data-flow diagram tagged with classification and encryption at every hop.
HomeworkDesign a "delete all my data" flow across 6 systems including backups and caches.
D120

Cost Engineering (FinOps)

30 min · Phase 08 · Prereq: Day 119

What the lesson covers

  • Unit economics — cost per request, per user, per GB stored
  • The big four cost drivers (compute, storage, egress, managed services) and where bills actually come from
  • Egress and cross-AZ traffic as silent killers
  • Right-sizing, spot/preemptible, reserved and savings plans
  • Storage tiering and lifecycle policies
  • Caching and compression as cost tools
  • Observability cost control (cardinality, retention, sampling)
  • Cost-aware architecture reviews
  • Showback/chargeback and tagging discipline
  • The cost/latency/reliability triangle
Whiteboard. a $40K/month bill broken down, with the three changes that cut it 60%.
HomeworkCompute cost per 1,000 requests for one of your services end to end.
