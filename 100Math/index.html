100 Days of Mathematical Prerequisites

The full mathematical foundation for machine learning, AI and quantitative work, from number sense to information theory, in 122 lessons. No code and no libraries: every result is stated precisely, every derivation is done by hand, and every lesson ends with worked examples and a proof for you to write yourself.

Phases

10 phases, in order. Each page holds every lesson of its phase.

PHASE 0 · DAYS 1–9

Number Sense & Algebraic Machinery

Phase 0 rebuilds the algebra that every later phase takes for granted, from the number systems and the rules they obey through divisibility, exponents and logarithms, factoring, partial fractions, inequalities, sums and the Binomial Theorem.

11 lessons · 5h 52m video PHASE 1 · DAYS 10–18

Functions, Geometry, Trigonometry & Complex Numbers

Phase 1 makes the function the central object: domains, inverses and transformations, then every standard family (polynomial, rational, exponential, logarithmic, trigonometric, hyperbolic) and the complex numbers that tie the exponential to rotation.

11 lessons · 5h 46m video PHASE 2 · DAYS 19–27

Logic, Sets, Proof & Combinatorics

Phase 2 teaches the language mathematics is written in: logic to read a theorem exactly, sets and relations to name the objects, proof techniques to establish claims, and combinatorics to count.

11 lessons · 5h 32m video PHASE 3 · DAYS 28–49

Linear Algebra

Phase 3 is the load-bearing pillar of the course: linear algebra from vectors and dot products up to least squares, eigendecomposition, the singular value decomposition and PCA, with every result derived by hand.

25 lessons · 14h 34m video PHASE 4 · DAYS 50–63

Single-Variable Calculus

Phase 4 is single-variable calculus done properly: limits from intuition to the ε–δ definition, derivatives and the theorems that make them trustworthy, Taylor series, and integration from Riemann sums to the Gaussian integral.

16 lessons · 8h 52m video PHASE 5 · DAYS 64–74

Multivariable & Vector Calculus

Phase 5 extends calculus from one variable to many: gradients, Jacobians and Hessians, the chain rule as matrix multiplication, optimization with and without constraints, and integration over regions, curves and surfaces.

13 lessons · 7h 22m video PHASE 6 · DAYS 75–81

Matrix Calculus & Optimization Theory

Phase 6 turns multivariable calculus into optimization theory: differentiating vector and matrix expressions without leaving matrix notation, recognizing convexity, analysing gradient descent and Newton's method, and solving constrained problems through dualit

8 lessons · 4h 42m video PHASE 7 · DAYS 82–91

Probability

Phase 7 builds probability from Kolmogorov's three axioms up to the limit theorems, deriving every distribution, expectation and inequality rather than memorising it.

13 lessons · 7h 52m video PHASE 8 · DAYS 92–97

Statistics & Inference

Phase 8 turns the probability of Phase 7 around: instead of computing what data a model produces, it infers the model from the data, deriving estimators, intervals, tests and linear regression from first principles rather than recipes.

9 lessons · 5h 10m video PHASE 9 · DAYS 98–100

Information Theory, Numerics & Capstone

Phase 9 adds the last two toolkits, information theory and numerical mathematics, previews the analysis and measure theory underneath everything, and closes the course with a full dependency map and a 40-problem comprehensive exam.

5 lessons · 2h 57m video

How every lesson is built

  • Core idea the one sentence the lesson exists to teach
  • Definitions & theorems stated with their hypotheses, then proved or derived
  • Worked examples the examples from the plan, solved step by step
  • Figures graphs and geometric pictures drawn as diagrams
  • Common errors the mistakes that cost marks and why they happen
  • Prove it the result you derive yourself, plus practice problems
