How to use these notes

Read the lecture note after attempting the derivation yourself, not before. The notes are written to be checkable: every theorem is stated with its hypotheses in force, and every proof sketch names the technique it uses so you can look up the full argument. Where a result is famous but the hypotheses are usually elided in courses, the note says which hypothesis is doing the work.

  • Theorem boxes are stated so that dropping a hypothesis produces a false statement. Where a counterexample is short, it is given.
  • Where it breaks boxes are the part that transfers to research. Most published mistakes come from applying a theorem outside its hypotheses, not from bad algebra.
  • Demos are numerical, not decorative. They are there so you can watch a rate be a rate, or watch a bound go vacuous.
  • Implementation stubs are NumPy-only and deliberately incomplete in the interesting places. Filling them in is the exercise.
Syllabus errata

The design notes claim four days exceed 3.5 h of lecture; the day tables mark three (Days 42, 65, 100 at 220, 230 and 250 min). Day 85 at 170 min and Days 82–85 as a block are the next-heaviest. Plan the split at Day 42, 65 and 100 regardless.

Module 8's stated dependency on “Module 9's early lectures” means Lectures 109–111 (priors, asymptotics, evidence). If you take the modules in printed order, read 109 and 111 before Lecture 105.

Prerequisites, and how to tell if you have them

The self-test below is a better filter than a list of course names. If more than two are uncomfortable, spend the two weeks the syllabus recommends before Day 1.

AreaSelf-test — can you do this cold?
Linear algebraShow that for A∈ℝn×p the matrix A(A⊤A)−1A⊤ is the orthogonal projector onto col(A), and read its eigenvalues off that fact.
CalculusDifferentiate logdetΣ+tr(Σ−1S) with respect to Σ and set it to zero.
ProbabilityState the difference between Xn→X in probability and almost surely, and give a sequence separating them.
Real analysisProve that a continuous function on a compact set attains its supremum, and say where compactness is used.
ProgrammingWrite ridge regression from an SVD in ten lines with no library beyond NumPy, and explain the condition number of what you inverted.
