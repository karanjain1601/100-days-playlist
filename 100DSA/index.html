100 Days of DSA & Competitive Programming

Data structures and algorithms for interviews and competitive programming, in Python, across 142 lessons. Every lesson states the idea, proves why it works and what it costs, builds the implementation you will keep in your library, and walks through real problems from CSES, Codeforces, AtCoder and LeetCode.

How every lesson is built

  • Core idea the one sentence the lesson exists to teach
  • Theory definitions, invariants, correctness arguments and complexity
  • Implementation contest-ready Python with the speed tricks that matter
  • Problems worked problems from the plan, from observation to accepted code
  • What breaks TLE, WA and RE traps, and Python-specific pitfalls
  • Practice the problem set to solve after the lesson
